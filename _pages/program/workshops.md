The following workshops have been accepted to be co-located with EACL 2027:

* The 8th Workshop on African Natural Language Processing (AfricaNLP)
* SilkRoadNLP 2027: Advancing NLP for the Iranian Language Family
* ORACLE: Second Workshop on Open Reasoning Across Cultures & Languages
* CHOMPS: Second Workshop on Confabulations, Hallucinations and Overgenerations in Multilingual and Precision-demanding Scenarios
* Field Matters: The Sixth Workshop on NLP Applications to Field Linguistics
* LInDA: Workshop on Linguistic Information for Downstream Applications
* ArgMining 2027: The 14th Workshop on Argument Mining and Reasoning
* The 23rd Workshop on Multiword Expressions
* VarDial 2027: The Fourteenth Workshop on NLP for Similar Languages, Varieties and Dialects
* The 1st Multidisciplinary Workshop on NLP for Democracies (MultiDems)
* The Third Workshop on Language Models for Low-Resource Languages
* Slavic NLP 2027
* SIGHUM (LaTeCH-CLfL) 2027: The 11th Joint SIGHUM Workshop on Computational Linguistics for Cultural Heritage, Social Sciences, Humanities and Literature

Further details about each workshop (organizers, descriptions, websites, and schedule) will be added soon.
