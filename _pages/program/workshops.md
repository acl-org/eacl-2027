### [CHOMPS: Second Workshop on Confabulations, Hallucinations and Overgenerations in Multilingual and Precision-demanding Scenarios](https://chomps-workshop.github.io/)
* **Organizers:** Aman Sinha, Chuyuan Li, Raúl Vázquez, Boya Zhang, Timothee Mickus, Nona Naderi, Jörg Tiedemann and Douglas Teodoro

### [Field Matters: The Sixth Workshop on NLP Applications to Field Linguistics](https://field-matters.github.io/)
* **Organizers:** Éric Le Ferrand, Elena Klyachko, Shu Okabe, Oleg Serikov, Ekaterina Voloshina and Karina Sheifer
* **Description:** The Field Matters workshop aims to bring together the urgent needs of field linguists and the vast community of NLP practitioners, developing up-to-date NLP tools for easier, faster, more reliable data collection and annotation.

### [LInDA: Workshop on Linguistic Information for Downstream Applications](https://linda-workshop.github.io/)
* **Organizers:** Marion Di Marco, Niyati Bafna, Aleksei Dorkin, Shu Okabe, Juan Antonio Pérez-Ortiz, Felipe Sánchez Martínez, Abishek Stephen, Tsedeniya Kinfe Temesgen and Alexander Fraser
* **Description:** Many languages remain underrepresented in large language models (LLMs), resulting in limited performance on downstream tasks. However, many languages have other resources: dictionaries, grammars, morphological analyses, and other forms of annotated texts. LInDA studies how linguistic information can improve downstream applications.

### [ArgMining 2027: The 14th Workshop on Argument Mining and Reasoning](https://argmining-org.github.io/)
* **Organizers:** Debanjan Ghosh, Kamila Gorska, Zlata Kikteva, Priya Pitre, Jodi Schneider, Manfred Stede and Michiel van der Meer
* **Description:** Argument Mining focuses on the development of large-scale argumentation datasets and tasks like argument quality assessment, argument persuasiveness, and the synthesis of argumentative texts, spanning domains such as legal, social, medical, political, and scientific settings. The 2027 edition of the ArgMining workshop has a special theme of Argumentation in Agentic and Human-AI Settings.

### [The 23rd Workshop on Multiword Expressions (MWE 2027)](https://multiword.org/mwe2027/)
* **Organizers:** Patricia Chiril, Jaka Čibej, Adriana Silvana Pagano, Ivelina Stoyanova and Sara Stymne
* **Description:** MWE is a well-established series of workshops focusing on various aspects of multiword expressions, from their linguistic description and annotation to the development of tools able to process, identify, recognize them in text.

### [VarDial 2027: The Fourteenth Workshop on NLP for Similar Languages, Varieties and Dialects](https://aclsigvar.org/vardial2027)
* **Organizers:** Verena Blaschke, Yves Scherrer, Niyati Bafna, Tommi Jauhiainen, Alan Ramponi, Nikola Ljubešić, Marcos Zampieri, Preslav Nakov and Jörg Tiedemann
* **Description:** VarDial is a well-established series of workshops promoting a forum for scholars working on a range of topics related to the study of diatopic language variation from a computational perspective. The workshop deals with computational methods and language resources for closely related languages, language varieties, and dialects.

### [The 1st Multidisciplinary Workshop on NLP for Democracies (MultiDems)](https://sites.google.com/view/multidems/home)
* **Organizers:** Salim Hafid, Pierre-Antoine Lequeu, Nazanin Shafiabadi, Eoghan Cunningham, Tanise Ceron, Laurène Cave, Carlo Santagiustina, Jean-Philippe Cointet, Paul Lerner, François Yvon, Benjamin Piwowarski and Gaël Lejeune
* **Description:** MultiDems aims to provide a dedicated forum for research at the intersection of NLP, political science, social science, computational social choice, and related disciplines. In particular, we encourage the submission of work that moves beyond predominantly US-centric, monolingual, or two-party settings and studies NLP systems in the linguistic, institutional, and political diversity of democratic systems worldwide.

### [Slavic NLP 2027](https://bsnlp.cs.helsinki.fi/)
* **Organizers:** Dimitar Dimitrov, Jakub Piskorski, Michał Marcińczuk, Arkadiusz Modzelewski, Preslav Nakov, Agata Savary and Roman Yangarber
* **Description:** The goal of this Workshop is to bring together researchers from academia and industry working on NLP for Slavic languages. In particular, the Workshop aims to stimulate research and foster the creation of tools and resources for these languages. The Workshop will provide a forum for exchanging ideas, discussing current problems, and making the available resources more widely known.

### [SIGHUM (LaTeCH-CLfL) 2027: The 11th Joint SIGHUM Workshop on Computational Linguistics for Cultural Heritage, Social Sciences, Humanities and Literature](https://sighum.wordpress.com/sighum-2027/)
* **Organizers:** Diego Alves, Yuri Bizzoni, Stefania Degaetano-Ortlieb, Anna Kazantseva, Janis Pagel and Stan Szpakowicz
* **Description:** SIGHUM (LaTeCH-CLfL) 2027 is the eleventh in a series of meetings for NLP researchers who work with data from the broadly understood arts, humanities and social sciences, and for specialists in those disciplines who apply NLP techniques in their work.

### [International FrameNet Workshop 2027: Frame-Based Language Resources and Large Language Models Revisited](https://www.globalframenet.org/ifnw-2027)
* **Organizers:** Tiago Torrent, Voula Giouli, Oliver Czulo, Kyoko Ohara and Haris Papageorgiou
* **Description:** The workshop aims to foster an interdisciplinary dialog on recent advances in frame semantics, FrameNets, and frame-based approaches to language and knowledge representation, as well as their applications and explainability power in contemporary NLP and AI.
* **Location:** This workshop will be held at a separate venue (off-site).
