The August ARR cycle, valid for EACL 2027, received a total of 4136 submissions. Out of these, 2932 submissions indicated EACL 2027 as the preferred venue. We took this number as the total over which we calculated the acceptance rates mentioned in the notification emails. This is imperfect, of course, but a good working proxy. The same was done for the latest EMNLP and it’s common practice for \*CL conferences.

Of the original 4136 papers, 699 (17%) were withdrawn (289) or desk rejected (410), leaving 3437 valid submissions to enter the whole reviewing pipeline.

After the review process was completed, authors could choose to commit their paper to EACL 2027. The number of committed papers was 1761. The largest proportion of these comes from the August cycle, but there are some from previous cycles as well. One could also consider this number the total over which acceptance rates are calculated.

After a couple more desk rejects (4) and withdrawals (5), 1752 papers were left to possibly enter the final EACL 2027 programme. Following the recommendations of the SACs, which were based on the ACs’ metareviews and the original reviews, the PC Chairs’ final decisions yielded this picture:

- **Reject:** 634
- **Accept:** 1118
  - **Main:** 397
  - **Findings:** 721

Over the original 2932 submissions which indicated EACL 2027 as preferred venue, thus not including the additional papers committed to EACL 2027 from previous cycles, but still including the desk rejects (283) and the withdrawals (168) and also the papers eventually not committed to EACL 2027, the acceptance percentages are as follows:

- **Main:** 13%
- **Findings:** 25%

Over the total committed papers (1761), the acceptance percentages are as follows:

- **Main:** 22%
- **Findings:** 41%

The relationship between submission and acceptance is obviously complicated, especially given our ARR- and Commitment-based system. We hope this information helps to provide a better understanding and interpretation of the rates!
